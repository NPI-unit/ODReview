NKReview · v0.4 · how the OD is measured

From a brightfield image to one optical density per cell

NKReview finds cells or nuclei in DAB-stained brightfield images, measures each one against the background right next to it, and reports the optical density the way the Fiji method does – plus the distribution of OD inside every cell.
